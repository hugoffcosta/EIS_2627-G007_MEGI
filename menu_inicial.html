04 / FAQS

Perguntas frequentes.

How can I book a transport service?

Register as a customer on our platform, then select “Book a Transportation” and enter your shipment details to complete your booking.

What information do I need to provide when booking?

Please provide the collection and delivery addresses, your preferred date, and the size, weight and type of goods.

How much does transport cost?

Use our price simulator to get an estimated price range. Register as a customer to receive the exact price for your shipment.

Where does TrustPort operate?

We provide transport services across Portugal. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific route.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes. You can follow your transport’s progress and receive delivery notifications through the TrustPort app.

How long will my delivery take?

Delivery times depend on the route and service availability. We provide an estimated timeframe when confirming your booking.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Contact us as soon as possible to request a change or cancellation. Available options and any charges depend on the status and conditions of your booking.

What happens if my delivery is delayed?

We will keep you informed of any changes and provide an updated delivery estimate. Our team is available if you need assistance.

Are there any items TrustPort cannot transport?

Some goods may require special handling or may not be accepted. Contact us before booking if your shipment includes hazardous, fragile, perishable or unusually large items.

What should I do if my goods arrive damaged?

Report the damage to our team as soon as possible. Include your booking reference, a description of the issue and photographs so we can assess the situation.

How can I contact TrustPort?

Email info@trustport.com or call +351 220 000 740. Our headquarters are at Rua do Horizonte, n.º 74, 4100-000 Porto, Portugal.
